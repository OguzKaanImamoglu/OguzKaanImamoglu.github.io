Master's thesis, TU Munich, 2025

Generalizing Tracking Models under Sim-to-Real Domain Shift for Guidewire Tracking in X-ray Fluoroscopy

Annotated clinical X-ray sequences are scarce. This thesis generates synthetic fluoroscopy sequences from CT angiography scans, with a moving guidewire and simulated cardiac motion, trains detection and tracking models on them, and closes the sim-to-real gap with an unsupervised domain adaptation pipeline based on domain augmentation and iterative pseudo-labelling.
